Highlights

  • Camera-based rPPG estimated heart rate within 2.9 bpm of ECG at rest in 112 participants.
  • Error increased to 4.6 bpm during moderate exercise and was highest under low illumination.
  • rPPG is suitable as an adjunct to, not a replacement for, contact sensors in home programmes.
